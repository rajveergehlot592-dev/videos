## 2. How to Pass YouTube Monetization (Avoid "Reused Content" Bans)
YouTube's #1 rejection reason for clip channels is **Reused Content** or **Repetitious / Automated Content**. Every Pro Reel we generated already includes transformative elements, and you should follow these rules on every upload:
1. **Always Keep Original Commentary / Voiceover ON**: Never upload raw silent stream cuts. Our `edge-tts` intro commentary + audio ducking + custom sound effects (`whoosh`, `bass_drop`, `pop`) establish clear transformative commentary. (Adding your own 3-second mic reaction on your top clips makes approval even stronger!)
2. **Never Upload Identical Templates Back-to-Back**: Vary the hook color, layout (`Blurred BG` vs `Dynamic Crop`), and voiceover angle across uploads.
3. **Avoid Copyrighted In-Game Radio Music**: If a clip has a licensed pop song playing loudly on a GTA 6 car radio, trim around it or duck the background music so Content ID doesn't claim the Short.
4. **Space Out Uploads (Do NOT Dump 16 Shorts in 1 Hour)**:
   - Upload **2–3 Shorts per day** at peak hours (e.g., **12:00 PM**, **5:30 PM**, **9:00 PM** local/target audience time). Dumping 15+ Shorts at once triggers YouTube's spam filter and tanks reach.

## 3. Differentiators Most Clip Channels Skip (High-Leverage Tactics)
- **"Related Video" Funnel**: Link each Short to a weekly 3-minute compilation ("Top 10 Craziest GTA 6 Moments This Week") via YouTube Studio's *Related Video* field to build **4,000 Watch Hours** alongside your **10M Shorts Views** path.
- **Engagement-Loop Pinned Comments**: Immediately pin a polarizing or debate-starting question (e.g., *"Wait—did he skip this on purpose or panic? 👇"*). Reply to the first 10 comments within 15 minutes of posting to boost early comment velocity.
- **Community Tab Polls (Daily)**: Post 1 GTA VI poll per day (e.g., *"Jason or Lucia—who has the better story missions so far?"*). Community polls get pushed to non-subscribers' Home feeds for free!
- **A/B Hook Testing**: If a Short gets `< 60%` Viewed vs. Swiped Away in the first 24 hours, keep the video live or re-test a sharper 2-word hook thumbnail (`"HE SKIPPED IT?!"`) on the next similar clip.

## 4. Daily Analytics Check Routine (5 Minutes/Day in YouTube Studio)
Check these 3 metrics every morning in **YouTube Studio → Analytics → Content → Shorts**:
1. **Viewed vs. Swiped Away** (Target: **> 70%** viewed). If < 60%, your first 1.5 seconds (hook + voiceover) need to be faster/punchier.
2. **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** (Target: **> 95%–110%** on 25s Shorts via loopable endings).
3. **Subscribers Gained per 1,000 Views** (Target: **> 3–5 subs/1k**). Double down on whichever clip category (e.g., `Physics Fails` vs `Cutscene Reactions`) brings the most subscribers!
