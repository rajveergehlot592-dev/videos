Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VicePulse Uploader handles data when connecting to Google and YouTube API Services.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize VicePulse Uploader via Google OAuth 2.0, the application requests access solely to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope in order to upload and schedule video content on your authorized YouTube channel.

2. How Data Is Used & Stored

  • OAuth refresh/access tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine solely to authenticate scheduled uploads.
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer any Google user data to third parties or advertisers.
  • We do not collect personal browsing history, contacts, or unrelated account information.

3. Google API Services & Revoking Access

VicePulse Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using this application, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke this application's access to your Google account at any time via the Google Security Settings page.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact rajveergehlot592@gmail.com.
